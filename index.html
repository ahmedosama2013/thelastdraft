The Unwritten are shielded by shadow. Burn it off with the beam, then finish them with a flare or a lamp. Streetlamps and cabin lights are safe ground: they restore the battery and keep your place. Red marks around your sight show where something is closing in. Loose margin notes lie off the road for those who go looking.
